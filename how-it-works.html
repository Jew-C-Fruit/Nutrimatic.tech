How it works

“When you get home” isn't good enough.

When it comes to supporting hard exercise, there are some things you need right away. That's why we're putting your supplement cabinet on the gym floor.

Place

Drop a bottle in the port.

Prime

A splash of water first.

Dose

Your blend, one cartridge at a time.

Mix

Water and whisk, bottom to rim.

Deliver

Yours, in about 45 seconds.

Wash

Then it cleans itself.

Scroll

Cartridges

The powder cartridge: sealed, ID chipped, swapped in seconds.

Each cartridge tells the machine what's inside and how to process it, while keeping track of expiration date and remaining stock.

The shake

500 ml, mixed to order.

Water is dosed to fill the bottle exactly, accounting for the powder's own volume, and the whisk works the whole column. Nothing sits pre-made in a cooler.

Ordering

Build your drink, pay by phone, mix it.

Pick your products and set each portion on the screen, hold your phone to the reader, and the machine takes it from there. Nothing to install to use the machine.

The app

Coached at the gym. Stocked at home.

Save your drinks and order ahead. Coachbot plans around today's session, works out what your diet is missing, and keeps your supplements at home topped up. Coming with the first machines. Get early access

Tell it what you're training.

Coachbot suggests a drink for before and after, with the price and points up front. Make it at the machine now, or get a reminder for later.

Save it once. Order ahead.

Save your blends and portions, order on your way over and pay in the app. At the machine, tap the screen and it pours.
